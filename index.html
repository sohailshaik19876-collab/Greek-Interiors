About Greek Interiors

Interiors With Intention.

Greek Interiors is a design and interior architecture studio built on a simple belief — a home should be shaped around the people who live in it, not around a trend.

We work closely with a small number of clients each year, giving every project the attention it deserves. From the first conversation, we study how you move through your day, how you host, how you rest, and how light travels through your space. That understanding becomes the brief — and everything from spatial planning to the grain of a veneer follows from it.

Our work brings together architecture, functionality, material honesty and lifestyle. We detail in-house, source responsibly, and stay on site until the last handle is aligned. The result is an interior that feels considered rather than decorated, and personal rather than styled.

Design Philosophy

Good design isn’t simply seen. It is experienced.

Six concerns sit behind every drawing we produce. They are the questions we return to when a decision is difficult — and the reason a Greek Interiors space feels calm long after the newness wears off.

  • iLightHow a room changes from morning to evening.
  • iiMaterialSurfaces that age well and feel right to touch.
  • iiiProportionScale, rhythm and the space between things.
  • ivFunctionDesign that quietly does its job every day.
  • vComfortWarmth, acoustics and ease of movement.
  • viCharacterThe detail that could only belong to you.

The Process

From Vision To Reality.

A clear, six-stage path with defined deliverables and approvals at every step — so you always know what is happening, and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understanding the client’s lifestyle, needs and aspirations. We visit the site, take stock of what exists, and agree on scope, timeline and budget before design begins.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Creating the overall visual and spatial direction. Mood, material palette and zoning come together into a single, coherent idea for the home.

  3. 03

    Design

    Developing layouts, materials, colours and detailed design. Joinery, lighting, electricals and plumbing are drawn to construction standard.

  4. 04

    Visualise

    Presenting realistic visualisations of the final space, with physical samples of every key finish so nothing is left to imagination.

  5. 05

    Execute

    Managing execution, coordination and finishing. Vetted vendors, weekly site reviews and a single point of contact throughout.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Delivering a completed space ready to experience — snag-free, styled, documented and backed by our post-handover support.
